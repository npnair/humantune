# HumanTune Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

HumanTune is a browser extension that skips AI-generated songs on the Spotify Web Player.

## What HumanTune does not do

- It does not collect, sell or share any personal information.
- It has no accounts, analytics, tracking or ads.
- It does not read your Spotify login, playlists or listening history beyond the song currently playing.

## Data stored on your device

HumanTune saves the following only in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). None of it is sent to the developer:

- Your on/off setting and other settings
- Artists and songs you marked "AI" or "Not AI"
- A list of the last 50 songs it skipped
- Cached artist lookups from MusicBrainz (kept for 7 days)

Removing the extension deletes all of this.

## Data sent to third parties

To estimate the AI score, HumanTune sends **the name of the artist currently playing** to the public [MusicBrainz API](https://musicbrainz.org/doc/MusicBrainz_API) (musicbrainz.org), an open music database. No other information about you is included. MusicBrainz's own privacy policy applies to those requests: https://metabrainz.org/privacy

If you choose to import a shared blocklist, HumanTune downloads that file from the GitHub or Gist URL you enter.
